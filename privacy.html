How we handle job-application information

This notice explains how Task Lane Company collects, uses, stores and deletes information submitted during recruitment.

1. Who controls the data

, located at , is the data controller for application information submitted through this site. Privacy questions may be sent to .

2. Information we collect

We collect information reasonably relevant to recruitment, such as your name, contact information, country/time zone, work history, availability, optional portfolio or professional-profile links, résumé/CV and application-source information. We do not ask for banking credentials, government ID numbers, medical information, political opinions, religious beliefs or other sensitive information in the initial application.

3. Why we use it

We use applicant information to administer recruitment, assess qualifications for the advertised role, communicate with applicants, prevent form abuse and maintain lawful recruitment records.

4. Service providers

We may use hosting, database, file-storage, anti-abuse and notification providers to operate recruitment. Access should be limited to authorized personnel and processors with a business need.

You may contact to ask about access, correction or deletion of applicant information. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.
