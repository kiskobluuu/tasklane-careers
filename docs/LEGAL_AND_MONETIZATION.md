# Legal + monetization launch checklist

This is an implementation checklist, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Job must be genuine
The role page and external job-board listing should state the actual employer, role, compensation/range where required, work arrangement, responsibilities and material conditions accurately. Do not describe an affiliate/referral opportunity as employment.

## Affiliate assessment
The safest architecture is already built into the site:
1. the work sample has a legitimate role-related purpose;
2. the compensated link is clearly disclosed beside the link;
3. an ordinary direct link is equally prominent;
4. a no-account alternative can be requested;
5. the form does not track which route the applicant uses;
6. no purchase or payment information is required;
7. the applicant is never asked to publish a positive review;
8. hiring decisions cannot be conditioned on creating a commissionable conversion.

Before activation, obtain written merchant/network approval that explicitly describes the traffic source as **applicants arriving from job-board listings and optionally evaluating the service as part of a hiring work sample**. Save the approval.

## Data protection
Collect only information necessary for recruitment. Publish the purpose, recipients/processors, retention period and contact method. Keep resumés private. Limit staff access. Honor applicable access/correction/deletion rights.

## Job-board rules
Review every board's current employer and off-site-application rules before posting. The public job listing should link to the job/application page, not an affiliate destination. Do not make the affiliate signup the headline or the economic substance of the role.

## Monetization areas ranked by risk
**Lowest risk:** optional resources shown after the application has been submitted, clearly marked as independent of hiring and compliant with merchant terms.

**Moderate:** an optional assessment tool with merchant approval, transparent compensation disclosure and a non-affiliate alternative.

**Highest / do not use:** mandatory affiliate conversion, requiring proof of signup, scoring candidates based on signup, disguising referral activity as employment, or using affiliate programs that prohibit incentivized/gig traffic.
